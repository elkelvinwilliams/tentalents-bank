Pitch us your project

Building something real? Tell us plainly.

Ten Talents backs projects and ventures with its own capital. We’re not a fund, we don’t take other people’s money, and we don’t want a pitch deck full of hockey sticks. We want to understand what you’re building, who pays for it, and why you’re the person to do it.

What we back

  • Property, products, services with paying customers
  • Early ventures with a founder doing the work
  • Projects in our communities with a real income model

How we invest

  • Our own capital only — no pooled money
  • Equity, revenue share or a loan — agreed case by case, in writing
  • Small first cheques; more when the numbers earn it

What we won’t

  • Promise you anything before due diligence
  • Ask you to send us money to invest
  • Rush. Good decisions take a few weeks
