Proposals

Building something of substance? Tell us plainly.

Ten Talents backs projects and ventures with its own capital and, where agreed, capital from investors or clients. We are not looking for optimistic projections. We want to understand what you are building, who pays for it, and why you are the person to build it.

What we back

  • Property, products and services with paying customers
  • Early ventures with a founder doing the work
  • Projects in our communities with a real income model

How we invest

  • Our own capital and, where agreed, investor or client capital
  • Equity, revenue share or a loan — agreed case by case, in writing
  • Modest first commitments; more when the numbers earn it

What we will not do

  • Promise you anything before due diligence
  • Ask you to send us money to invest
  • Hurry. Sound decisions take a few weeks
