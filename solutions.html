What we do

Ways we help your money grow.

Start with one thing, or let us look after the whole picture — it's entirely up to you. Wherever you're starting from, there's a place for you here.

Private Wealth Management

Discretionary and advisory portfolios built around your objectives. We handle asset allocation, manager selection, rebalancing and risk — and report on every decision with complete transparency.

  • Discretionary & advisory mandates
  • Multi-currency, multi-jurisdiction

Investment Advisory

Direct access to our investment committee and global research. We construct multi-asset strategies spanning public markets, private equity, real assets, hedge strategies and fixed income.

  • Global multi-asset research
  • Tactical & strategic positioning

Family Office Services

The administrative backbone of significant wealth: consolidated reporting across all your holdings, governance frameworks, cash management, and coordination with your lawyers and accountants.

  • Consolidated multi-bank reporting
  • Governance & family councils

Private & Lombard Lending

Liquidity without disrupting your long-term strategy. Borrow against your portfolio, property or other assets on bespoke terms — for opportunities, tax events or major purchases.

  • Lombard & property-backed credit
  • Flexible, relationship-based terms

Estate & Succession

Protecting your intentions across generations. We coordinate trusts, structures and succession plans that reduce friction, preserve value and honour your wishes for those who follow.

  • Trust & structure coordination
  • Intergenerational planning

Philanthropy & Impact

Capital with conscience. We help establish foundations, design giving strategies and build impact-aligned portfolios so your wealth advances the causes that define your legacy.

  • Foundation & trust set-up
  • Impact & ESG mandates
