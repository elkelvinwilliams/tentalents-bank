The Ten Talents Academy

Learn. Understand. Simulate. Then decide.

The Academy is the app. It begins with ten questions, gives you a readiness profile and a recommended track, then takes you from money foundations to markets, risk and building something of your own — with quizzes that teach, a risk-first simulator, a journal, a wisdom library and personal savings goals. Learning is gamified; trading never is. The first lesson is free.

How it works

Four tracks, taken in order, worked through rather than skimmed.

01

Money Foundations

Income, spending, saving, debt, compounding and inflation — the machinery underneath everything else.

02

How Markets Work

What is actually being bought and sold, who the participants are, and why prices move. First lesson free for everyone.

03

Understanding Risk

Why most short-term traders lose, what leverage really does, and how to recognise when you are being sold to.

04

Building Something

Entrepreneurship as a talent developed: skills, small bets, first customers, and the fundamentals that compound.

The app — in development

Practise before the market charges for the lesson.

Beyond the lessons: a readiness assessment, a demo simulator that shows money at risk before upside, a journal that shows you your own behaviour, and a tutor that explains but never advises.

  • Levels, streaks and badges — for lessons and discipline, never for trade frequency
  • Position-size, leverage and spread calculators within the reading
  • Scenario drills that practise judgement, not prediction
  • A wisdom library — the economics of scripture, studied as interpretation
What sets it apart

Built to be finished, not merely started.

Quizzes that teach

Every answer comes with an explanation of why it is right. Pass at 70% to unlock the next module, and retake as often as you like; the explanations are the lesson.

Progress that is kept

Your place, your scores and your streaks are saved. Learn on the train, and resume where you stopped.

Certificates in your name

Finish a track and download the certificate. It is an educational programme’s certificate — we say plainly that it is not a regulated qualification.
