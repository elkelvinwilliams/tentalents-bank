The roadmap

What’s open, and what waits — on purpose.

  • Open now: our own investing, the ventures above, the Academy, and the free community.
  • After compliance review: sharing selected opportunities with members, and automated trading tools — both in development, launching only once our legal work signs them off.
  • After FCA authorisation: managing client capital. This is the destination we’re building toward — properly, or not at all. Until then, nobody at Ten Talents will ever ask you to send us money to invest, and anyone claiming otherwise is not us.
Why this order

Proof before promises.

Anyone can promise returns. We’d rather show our working: invest our own capital, build real businesses, teach what we actually do — and earn the authorisation to do more. When we publish numbers, they’ll be audited ones.

The honest bit, as always

Nothing on this page is an invitation to invest with us or a recommendation to invest in anything. Our ventures are our risk, taken with our money. Investing puts capital at risk — ours included.
