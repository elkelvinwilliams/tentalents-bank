Education

Financial understanding, taught properly.

Four tracks, built in order, free to start. We publish as we write — honestly marked below. No jargon for its own sake, no “one weird trick”, and risk discussed like adults.

Open the Academy Four tracks, seventeen lessons, quizzes and certificates.

01

Money Foundations

Income, spending, saving, debt, compounding, inflation — the machinery underneath everything else. Where everyone should start, including people who think they already have. In the Academy now.

02

How Markets Work

What stocks, bonds, funds and currencies actually are, who the players are, why prices move, and what the industry doesn’t bother explaining to newcomers. In the Academy now — first lesson free.

03

Understanding Risk

The track most finance content skips, because it’s bad for sales. Why most short-term traders lose money, what leverage really does, how to think about what you can afford to lose, and how to recognise when you’re being sold to. In the Academy now.

04

Building Something

Entrepreneurship as a talent developed, not a lottery ticket: skills, small bets, first customers, and the boring fundamentals that actually compound. In the Academy now.

Wisdom

The economics of scripture.

Biblical Mysteries and Biblical Wealth: the lesser-discussed money in the text — preparation, debt, ownership, generosity, power. For the reader who wants the deeper things. Curious, never sensational; interpretations shown as interpretations; nothing claims scripture predicts prices.

01

The Economics of Joseph

Seven years of plenty, seven of famine — a 20% reserve and the first recorded counter-cyclical policy. What is your reserve?

02

The Parable of the Talents

Capital entrusted, stewardship measured, accountability demanded. Our origin story — a moral teaching, not market guidance.

03

Jubilee

Debt, land and ownership reset on a fifty-year cycle. What a society decides about debt says about what it values.

04

The Widow’s Oil

Scarcity, one asset, and the mathematics of multiplication.

05

Solomon’s Economy

Wisdom, trade, taxation and the price of concentrated wealth.

06

The Rich Fool

Accumulation meets mortality — the parable that prices time.
