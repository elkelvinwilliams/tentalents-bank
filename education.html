Education

Financial understanding, taught properly.

Four tracks, built in order and free to begin. We publish as we write, and each track is marked below. No jargon for its own sake, no “one weird trick”, and risk discussed as adults discuss it.

Open the Academy

Four tracks, seventeen lessons, quizzes and certificates.

01

Money Foundations

Income, spending, saving, debt, compounding and inflation — the machinery underneath everything else. Where everyone should start, including those who believe they already have. In the Academy now.

02

How Markets Work

What stocks, bonds, funds and currencies actually are, who the participants are, why prices move, and what the industry rarely troubles to explain to newcomers. In the Academy now — first lesson free.

03

Understanding Risk

The track most finance content omits, because it is bad for sales. Why most short-term traders lose money, what leverage really does, how to think about what you can afford to lose, and how to recognise when you are being sold to. In the Academy now.

04

Building Something

Entrepreneurship as a talent developed, not a lottery ticket: skills, small bets, first customers, and the unglamorous fundamentals that actually compound. In the Academy now.

Wisdom

The economics of scripture.

Biblical Mysteries and Biblical Wealth: the less-discussed money in the text — preparation, debt, ownership, generosity and power. For the reader who wants the deeper things. Curious, never sensational; interpretations are shown as interpretations, and nothing claims that scripture predicts prices.

01

The Economics of Joseph

Seven years of plenty, seven of famine — a 20% reserve and the first recorded counter-cyclical policy. What is your reserve?

02

The Parable of the Talents

Capital entrusted, stewardship measured, accountability required. Our origin story — a moral teaching, not market guidance.

03

Jubilee

Debt, land and ownership reset on a fifty-year cycle. What a society decides about debt reveals what it values.

04

The Widow’s Oil

Scarcity, one asset, and the mathematics of multiplication.

05

Solomon’s Economy

Wisdom, trade, taxation and the price of concentrated wealth.

06

The Rich Fool

Accumulation meets mortality — the parable that prices time.

Studies are published in the app as they are written. Study 01 is complete.
