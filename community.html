The rules

The same in every channel.

  • No financial advice between members. Share how things work, never what someone should buy.
  • No signals, tips, or “guaranteed” anything. Posting them is an immediate removal.
  • No invitations to invest. Ten Talents never asks anyone to send money to invest in any channel. Anyone who does is not us.
  • Respect the room. Disagree with ideas, never at people.
