The rules

A code of conduct we enforce.

  • No financial advice between members. Share how things work, never what someone should buy.
  • No signals, tips, or “guaranteed” anything. Posting them is an immediate removal.
  • No selling to members. No DM pitching, no recruitment, no affiliate links.
  • Respect the room. Disagree with ideas, never at people. We remove fast and don’t argue about it.
