House rules

The same in every channel.

  • No financial advice between members. Share how things work, never what someone should buy.
  • No signals, tips or “guaranteed” anything. Posting them leads to immediate removal.
  • No investment offers between members. Members may not ask others to send money or to invest.
  • Respect the room. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
