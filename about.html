About

Built on an old idea about what you do with what you’re given.

In the parable we’re named after, three people are entrusted with money — “talents”. Two put theirs to work and grow it. One buries his in the ground, afraid of losing it. The lesson isn’t about money. It’s that ability, knowledge and opportunity are given to be developed — and that fear and ignorance are how they get buried.

The story

Why this exists.

Most people are never taught how money works. The gap gets filled by two industries: one that makes finance feel impossibly complicated, and one that makes it sound impossibly easy. Both profit from you not understanding.

Ten Talents was founded in 2026 by a UK-based founder building it full-time, in the open, to be the third thing: a community where ambitious people learn how money, markets and business actually work — honestly, patiently, and together.

We’re new. We’d rather grow slowly and be trusted than grow fast and be another reason people roll their eyes at finance content.

Plainly

What we are. What we are not.

Ten Talents is

  • A community and an education platform
  • General financial literacy — how things work
  • Honest about risk, difficulty and our own stage
  • Free to join, with paid tiers to come

Ten Talents is not

  • Financial advice, or a place that tells you what to buy
  • A signals group, tips channel or “funded account” scheme
  • A get-rich-quick anything
  • A bank, broker or investment firm
Follow the money

How we’ll make money — and why we’ll tell you.

Today: we don’t. Joining is free and there’s nothing to buy. As we grow, revenue will come from paid membership tiers, educational programmes and events — things you pay for knowingly, priced plainly.

In time we may also introduce members to carefully vetted commercial partners, and be paid for those introductions. Before that ever happens, we’re doing the legal and compliance work — because introducing people to financial services is regulated territory, and we’d rather be slow than sorry. When it launches, every such relationship will be disclosed on the page where you see it.

That’s the deal: we tell you how we’re paid, and we never let a commission decide what we teach.
