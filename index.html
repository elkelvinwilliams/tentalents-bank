How we decide

What we look for. What we never do.

  • Real customers, or a clear and honest path to them
  • A founder we’d still back in a downturn
  • Numbers that add up without a story doing the heavy lifting
  • A stewardship ethic — the money is a trust, not a trophy
  • An income or an exit we actually understand

We don’t take your money to invest.

Not as an investment, a loan, a “buyer” arrangement or anything else — until we hold the right authorisation, confirmed in writing. We don’t promise returns and we don’t publish numbers we can’t prove. If anyone claiming to be Ten Talents asks you to send money to invest, it isn’t us.

Invest alongside us

Not yet. And we’ll say so plainly when that changes.

Many people ask to invest with Ten Talents. The honest answer today is no — not until we hold the authorisation or a lawful route confirmed in writing by our advisers. We’re doing that work properly. When it’s done, it will be announced here, in writing, with every risk spelled out.
